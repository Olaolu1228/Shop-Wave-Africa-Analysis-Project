# ShopWave Africa: E-commerce Revenue & Operations Analysis

## Project Overview

ShopWave Africa is a rapidly growing e-commerce marketplace operating across Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya, offering products across Electronics, Fashion, Home Appliances, Beauty, and Grocery categories.

Despite achieving strong revenue growth, the business faced declining profitability, rising customer complaints, and unreliable reporting caused by poor data quality. This project focused on transforming raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence by cleaning and validating the dataset, uncovering operational inefficiencies, and building an executive dashboard for strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The primary goals of this analysis were to:

* Identify sources of revenue leakage and profit loss
* Analyze order cancellations and delivery performance
* Evaluate customer retention and purchasing behavior
* Assess product and regional performance
* Improve reporting accuracy through data quality validation
* Deliver an executive dashboard to support data-driven decisions

* Standardization of date formats and categorical fields
* Creation of delivery, return, discount, revenue, and profit metrics

## Key Insights

### Revenue Leakage Analysis

Analysis revealed that approximately **$784,006 (13.4% of total revenue)** was at risk due to:

* Returns: **$388,756**
* Discounts: **$308,518**
* Loss-making orders: **$86,732**

A 20% reduction in returns alone could potentially recover more than **$77,000** in revenue.

### Cancellation Analysis

* Fashion products recorded the highest cancellation rate at **7.33%**
* The East region experienced the highest cancellation rate at **5.91%**
* Delivery delays showed a strong correlation with order cancellations

### Customer Retention Analysis

Customer loyalty was exceptionally strong:

* Repeat purchase rate reached **94%**
* Customers with **4–6 orders generated 52.5% of total revenue**
* The highest-spending customer generated over **$13,000** across 10 orders

These findings highlighted the importance of customer retention and loyalty-focused marketing initiatives.

### Delivery Performance

* Average delivery time was approximately **5 days**
* Electronics experienced the longest delivery times
* The East region consistently underperformed compared to other regions
* North and Central regions demonstrated the highest operational efficiency

### Product Performance

* The top-selling product generated over **$13,000** in revenue
* Several products generated minimal sales and low customer demand
* Products **221459** and **245799** recorded a **100% return rate**, indicating potential quality or fulfillment issues
